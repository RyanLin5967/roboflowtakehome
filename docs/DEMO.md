1. **0:00–1:00:** Show the question that must change: which side of the cup is the bottle on? Place cup/bottle before a stationary camera. Roboflow supplies detections; the recipe evaluates their relationship.
2. **1:00–3:00:** Save first image; deliberately move the cup as well as bottle. Show targeted repair and preserved first capture. Restore reference and capture counterpart. Use live only after rehearsal; illustrated replay remains labeled.
3. **3:00–4:00:** Reload partway through collection. Saved work survives, but a new scene check is necessary because restoring database state cannot restore the physical world. Finish/download.
4. **4:00–5:00:** Two opposite-order sequences with identical start/end arrangements. Answer depends on time, not final frame. Observed intervals support arrival order; uncertain order/occlusion causes refusal.
5. **5:00–7:00:** Show history and archive: original bytes, hashes/times, detections, controls, verifier version, paired JSONL and shared split. Preview advises; it cannot approve a saved capture.
6. **7:00–8:00:** Run bounded infrastructure replay or show its report:240 previews→12 actionable pending jobs. Explain actual subprocess-crash tests, fences, and why remote work may repeat while local acceptance cannot.
7. **8:00–9:00:** Real discovered failure: a successful Roboflow upload was temporarily invisible to search. Persisted read-only backoff handles this without reuploading. Show generated-card transport test separately from collector evidence.
8. **9:00–10:00:** Questions/limits: detector errors, approximate controls, class-distinct objects, untrusted browser provenance and calibration. A concrete collection instrument and reliable data path, not a new detector or proven labeling-cost reduction.
